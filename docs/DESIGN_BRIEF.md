# Design Brief

This is the original creative brief used to generate the Rajadhaniyam
storefront in Lovable. Moved here from `README.md` during the Phase 1
architecture restructuring so the README can serve its normal role
(setup/run instructions) while this reference is preserved — it's still the
source of truth for the storefront's visual language when building new
pages or admin UI.

---

# Rajadhaniyam Heritage

Create a completely redesigned premium e-commerce website UI/UX for:

RAJADHANIYAM

"Our Tradition, Your Health"

I have attached:

1. The Rajadhaniyam logo
2. Existing website/reference screenshots from the client

Use the logo as the brand identity.

The screenshots are ONLY references for the client's existing content and product structure.

DO NOT copy their visual design.

Create a significantly more premium, sophisticated and memorable D2C website.

## Design Concept

Create the visual identity around:

"INDIAN HERITAGE × MODERN WELLNESS × PREMIUM FOOD"

The website should feel like a premium Indian natural-food brand that could confidently compete with high-end D2C food and wellness brands.

The design should communicate: Heritage, Purity, Authenticity, Premium quality, Natural ingredients, Trust, Modern lifestyle.

Avoid making it look like a typical millet/organic-food Shopify website.

## Color Palette

Use a completely different palette from the typical green-heavy food websites.

- **Primary — Deep Olive**: `#303B24`
- **Accent — Terracotta**: `#A6533A`
- **Premium Accent — Champagne Gold**: `#D6B878`
- **Main Background — Warm Ivory**: `#F7F3E8`
- **Secondary Background — Soft Sand**: `#E8DDC8`
- **Text — Deep Charcoal**: `#24241F`
- **White**: `#FFFDF8`

IMPORTANT: Do NOT make green the dominant color across every section.

- Warm Ivory + White as the majority of the interface.
- Deep Olive for important brand sections, navigation accents and CTAs.
- Terracotta very selectively for highlights, badges and small visual accents.
- Champagne Gold for premium details and decorative elements.

The result should feel warm, sophisticated and luxurious.

## Typography

Elegant editorial serif for major headings — Cormorant Garamond or Playfair Display.

Clean modern sans-serif for UI — Manrope or Inter.

Typography should feel editorial and premium: large elegant headings,
generous line height, small uppercase eyebrow labels, clean product
typography, strong price hierarchy.

## Header

Premium two-level header.

**Top bar** — Left: "Our Tradition, Your Health" · Center: "Free Shipping
on Orders Above ₹499" · Right: "Track Order", "Customer Support"

**Main header** — Left: Rajadhaniyam logo · Nav: Home, Shop, Millets, Nuts
& Seeds, Combo Packs, About Us, Contact · Right: Search, Account, Cart

Clean ivory/white header with a subtle champagne-gold detail. Sticky on scroll.

## Hero

High-end editorial hero, split composition.

**Left** — Label: "ROOTED IN INDIAN TRADITION" · Headline: "Nourishing
Traditions, Healthy Generations" · Copy: "Wholesome millets, grains and
naturally sourced foods crafted for healthier everyday living." · CTA:
"Shop the Collection →" · Secondary: "Our Story" · Below CTA: 100% Natural,
Rich in Fibre, Carefully Sourced.

**Right** — Premium still-life: millet grains, millet flour, nuts, wooden
bowls, natural leaves, wheat/millet stalks, premium product packaging.
Warm natural lighting, ivory/soft-sand background, subtle terracotta
elements. Should feel like a premium food magazine advertisement.

## Decorative Language

Very subtle Indian-inspired visual elements: fine botanical line drawings,
millet/wheat illustrations, thin decorative borders, small ornamental
separators, handcrafted visual details. Do NOT use excessive traditional
motifs — keep everything modern.

## Shop by Category

Heading: "Explore Our Collection" · Subheading: "Ancient grains and
wholesome ingredients, thoughtfully selected for modern living."

Five categories: Millet Grains, Millet Flours, Millet Ready Mix, Nuts &
Seeds, Combo Packs.

Premium photography, editorial product-story feel. Card: large image,
category, short description, "Explore →". Soft ivory cards, subtle
champagne borders. Hover: image zoom, arrow movement, subtle elevation.

## Brand Values

Full-width deep olive section. Heading: "Goodness You Can Trust". Values:
Traditional & Authentic, Carefully Sourced, No Added Preservatives,
Hygienically Packed, Supports Local Farmers. Elegant thin-line icons,
champagne accents.

## Bestsellers

Heading: "From Our Pantry to Yours" · Subheading: "Our most-loved everyday
essentials."

Products: Kambu Broken, Foxtail Millet (Thinai), Raagi Whole, Jowar Flour,
Roasted Pistachios, Millet Healthy Mix.

Six premium product cards: image, small category, name, weight, price, Add
to Cart. Warm ivory backgrounds, no excessive rounding, photography-first.

## Product Card

Premium food packaging/catalogue card style: image, name, weight, price,
Add to Cart. Subtle borders, no heavy shadows, no excessive badges. Tiny
terracotta "Bestseller" label where appropriate.

## Story Section

Large editorial section. Left: "Our Tradition" — "At Rajadhaniyam, we bring
the goodness of ancient grains and natural foods to your table. Rooted in
tradition, we believe everyday nourishment should be simple, pure and
thoughtfully sourced." CTA: "Discover Our Story →"

Right: large premium lifestyle image. Overlay three stats: 5000+ Happy
Customers, 4.8★ Customer Rating, 100+ Healthy Products.

## Editorial Promotional Section

Full-width terracotta background. Heading: "Rediscover the Goodness of
Ancient Grains" · Text: "Simple ingredients. Timeless nourishment." · CTA:
"Shop Millets →". Cream typography, subtle botanical/grain illustrations.
Visually striking but sophisticated.

## Trust Strip

Minimal ivory section, four columns: Free Shipping (orders above ₹499),
Secure Payments (safe & secure checkout), Easy Returns (hassle-free
returns), Customer Support (+91 XXXXX XXXXX). Elegant line icons.

## Shop Page

"Shop" — "Wholesome choices for everyday living." Category navigation,
product grid. Filters: Category, Price, Availability. Sort: Featured,
Price Low to High, Price High to Low. Keep it simple.

## Product Page

Left: large image gallery. Right: category, name, rating, price, MRP,
discount, weight selector, quantity selector, Add to Cart / Buy Now.
Information: Description, Ingredients, Nutritional Information, Shipping
Information. Below: Related Products.

## Cart

Premium cart drawer and cart page. Show: product, image, weight, quantity,
price. Summary: subtotal, shipping, total. CTA: Proceed to Checkout. Note:
"Free shipping on orders above ₹499".

## Checkout

Very clean checkout. Customer: name, mobile, email. Delivery: address,
city, state, pincode. Order summary. Payment: Online Payment, Cash on
Delivery. Primary CTA: Place Order. Avoid distractions.

## Order Success

"Thank You for Your Order" — "Your order has been placed successfully."
Show: Order ID, Order Amount, Delivery Information. Buttons: Track Order,
Continue Shopping.

## Admin Panel

Separate admin dashboard with a completely different visual language from
the customer website.

- Customer website: editorial + premium + warm.
- Admin: modern SaaS + clean + efficient.

### Admin Login

Minimal login page: Rajadhaniyam logo, "Welcome Back", Email, Password,
Sign In.

### Admin Sidebar

Dashboard, Products, Categories, Orders, Settings. Deep olive accent.

### Admin Dashboard

Show: Total Orders, Total Sales, Products, Low Stock (e.g. 128 Orders,
₹84,520 Sales, 42 Products, 6 Low Stock). Clean sales chart. Recent Orders
table: Order ID, Customer, Date, Amount, Payment, Status.

### Admin Products

Header: "Products" + "Add Product". Table: Product, Category, Price,
Stock, Status, Actions. Add/Edit form: Name, Description, Category, Price,
MRP, Stock, Weight, Image, Status.

### Admin Categories

Millet Grains, Millet Flours, Millet Ready Mix, Nuts & Seeds, Combo Packs.
Actions: Add, Edit, Delete.

### Admin Orders

Table: Order ID, Customer, Date, Items, Amount, Payment Status, Order
Status. Detail: customer info, delivery address, products, quantity,
subtotal, shipping, total, payment status, order status. Order statuses:
Pending, Confirmed, Shipped, Delivered, Cancelled.

## Responsive Design

Mobile-first. Mobile header: Logo, Search, Cart, Menu. Product grid: 2
columns. Hero stacks beautifully. Excellent spacing, touch-friendly
controls.

## Interaction

Subtle premium interactions: smooth hover transitions, product image zoom,
button micro-interactions, navigation animations, cart animation, scroll
reveal, sticky header, smooth category interactions. Do not over-animate.

## Scope Guardrails (MVP)

This is a premium but SIMPLE e-commerce MVP. Do NOT add: Wishlist, Loyalty
program, Advanced coupon engine, Multi-vendor, CRM, AI recommendations,
Subscription, Complex inventory, Multi-warehouse, Advanced analytics,
Complex customer accounts. Focus on making the defined pages extremely
polished.

> Note (Phase 1 restructuring): the codebase now _prepares_ for inventory,
> coupons and admin analytics as part of the platform architecture (see
> `docs/DEVELOPMENT_ROADMAP.md`) — this guardrail was about the original
> UI/UX scope, not a ban on the backend eventually supporting them.

## Final Design Standard

The attached client references look basic — this design must look at least
one major level above them. Final impression: "Premium Indian food brand
with strong heritage and modern D2C positioning."

Prioritize: premium photography, typography, spacing, color harmony,
editorial layouts, product presentation, brand storytelling,
conversion-focused UX, mobile experience, professional admin dashboard.

Do not make every section identical — alternate editorial sections,
product sections, full-width brand sections, photography sections, minimal
information sections.

Build the complete clickable UI mockup for both the customer website and
the admin panel.
